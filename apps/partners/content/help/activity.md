# The Activity page: your organisation's live feed

The **Activity** page (in the left sidebar) is a single place to see what is happening across your whole organisation — every booking, event registration and membership purchase, plus a calendar of how busy each day is and which memberships are starting or ending soon. Think of it as your notification board: open it in the morning and you know what happened overnight and what's coming up.

Everything on the page respects the timezone selected in the top bar.

## Recent activity feed

The **Recent activity** table lists who booked what, newest first. Each row shows:

- **When** — the moment the booking or purchase was made.
- **Customer** — the customer's name and contact (phone or email), when available.
- **Type** — one of:
  - **Booking** — a court/arena slot booking (walk-in, your venue site, Circls, or an aggregator).
  - **Event** — a registration for one of your events.
  - **Membership** — a purchase of one of your membership plans.
- **Item** — the court booked (or *Multiple courts* for a single booking spanning several), the event name, or the membership plan (with its tier underneath).
- **Venue** — which venue it belongs to. Org-wide events and memberships show a dash.
- **Scheduled** — when the booked session or event takes place; for memberships, the validity window (start – end date).
- **Status** — Pending, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled or No-show.
- **Total** — the amount paid, in rupees.

### Filtering and export

Use the controls above the table to narrow the feed by **type**, **venue**, a **date range** (when the booking was made), or a **customer** name/contact search. **Download CSV** exports the currently loaded rows with the active filters applied. The feed loads 50 rows at a time — use **Load more** at the bottom for older activity.

## Bookings calendar

The **Bookings calendar** shows a month at a glance:

- A day is **green** when it has at least one session scheduled (slot bookings and events; cancellations and no-shows don't count).
- The **number under the date** is that day's booking count.
- Use the arrows to move between months, and the venue filter in the feed to scope the calendar to one venue.
- **Click a day** to filter the feed below to the sessions taking place that day; click it again to clear.

The calendar counts sessions by the day they *take place*, not the day they were booked — so a booking made today for next Friday lights up next Friday.

## Memberships starting & ending

The **Memberships starting & ending** panel lists member subscriptions whose validity window opens or closes around now (from 7 days ago up to 30 days ahead):

- **Starting** — memberships that recently began or are about to begin.
- **Ending** — memberships approaching their end date (shown in amber), a good prompt to nudge the member about renewing.

Each entry shows the member, the plan and tier, and the full validity window. Cancelled memberships are not shown.
