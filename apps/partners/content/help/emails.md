# Emails circls sends you

circls emails your organisation about the things that need a person: a new booking, the outcome of a listing review, a customer question, a team invitation. Nothing here needs setting up — but **where** the emails go is up to you.

## Where the emails go

- If your [organisation profile](/help/organisation) has a **contact email**, every organisation email goes there (and only there).
- If it's empty, the emails go to the account email of each **Owner** on your team.

So: set a contact email if you'd rather a shared inbox (front desk, bookings@…) got them than the owners' personal addresses.

## What you'll receive

| Email | When | Who it goes to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New booking** | A customer books one of your arenas, events or membership plans through circls (the website, the app, a partner site, or your own website via the API). Bookings your team enters at the desk don't echo back to you. | Organisation contact (see above) |
| **Listing approved** | The circls team approves a venue, arena, event or membership plan you submitted. It's live for customers from that moment. | Organisation contact |
| **Listing needs changes** | The circls team can't approve a listing. The email lists what to fix. A listing that wasn't approved can't be edited back into review: for an event, **Duplicate** it, make the fixes and submit the copy; for a venue, arena or membership plan, add it again with the fixes. | Organisation contact |
| **New question** | A customer asks a question on one of your listings — see [Answering customer questions](/help/questions). | Organisation contact email only (nothing is sent if it's empty) |
| **Team invitation** | Someone on your team invites a person to join your organisation. | The invited person |

## What your customers receive

For every booking made through circls the customer is emailed (and sent an SMS when SMS is switched on and we have their number) — you don't need to tell them yourself:

- **Booking confirmed** — what they booked, when, and what they paid.
- **Booking cancelled** — when they cancel, or your team cancels it from the portal. The email says who cancelled, and the refund amount if one was issued with the cancellation.
- **Refund** — a receipt for every refund, with the amount, the reason and when to expect it. A refund issued with a cancellation sends this as well as the cancellation email.
- **Booking moved** — when your team reschedules a court booking.

**Cancelling an event** emails everyone holding a ticket for it (or for any date of a cancelled series). Their bookings are not cancelled or refunded automatically: the email tells them the refund comes from your organisation, so refund each registration from the event's registrations (or the booking's refund page).
