Welcome to circls! This guide walks you through the onboarding wizard — creating your organisation, then choosing what to set up first: **an event**, **a membership** or **a venue**. The whole flow takes about five minutes.

## Before you start

You'll need a circls Partner account. If a colleague invited you, follow your invite link instead — you'll join their organisation directly and can skip step 1 (see [Inviting and managing team members](/help/team)).

When you sign up with an email and password we send a **verification link** to that address. You can use the portal right away, but until you click the link a banner reminds you, and your email isn't shown to teammates or used to match your account (for example when someone invites that address). If it didn't arrive, check your spam or junk folder first, then use **Resend email** in the banner. Each resend replaces the previous link, so always open the **newest** email — an older link shows "expired or already used". Once you've clicked the link, come back to the portal tab and the banner clears on its own (or press **I've verified**).

If you step away mid-way — **Back**, a link, a reload — the wizard keeps your place and what you'd typed in the current step, so coming back picks up where you were (a note says so; **Start over** clears that step's form after asking). The Terms box is the one thing you tick again. All of it is kept only in this browser, for a week after your last change. Your place is dropped when you leave the wizard for the dashboard, open the event or plan you've just saved, or sign out; leaving for the schedule builder keeps it, so **Back** returns you to the last step. If you're no longer a member of the organisation you were setting up, or you've switched to another organisation since, the wizard starts from the beginning — and **Add organisation** (in the organisation menu at the top) always does. If the Partner Terms have changed since you started, you're asked to accept the new version before carrying on.

## Step 1 — Create your organisation

Your **organisation** is the top-level account that owns all of your events, memberships, venues, bookings and team members.

1. Enter an **Organisation name** (for example, *Greenfield Sports Club*).
2. A **URL slug** is suggested automatically from the name. You can edit it — it may contain only lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens, and must be unique. If it's taken, you'll see *"That URL slug is already taken — try another."*
3. Pick **where your organisation is based** (India or United States). This selects the [Partner Terms & Conditions](/help/terms-and-conditions) that apply to your organisation and the currency your customers pay in (₹ via Razorpay or Cashfree in India, $ via Stripe in the US).
4. Read the Terms shown for your region and tick **"I am authorised to act for this organisation and accept the Partner Terms & Conditions on its behalf"** — you can't create the organisation without accepting.
5. Click **Create & continue**.

Your organisation is created immediately and you become its **owner**, and your acceptance of the Terms (version, time and accepting user) is recorded against the organisation. This is the only step you can't skip — everything else can be added later.

## Step 2 — Choose what to set up first

Pick the one that fits how you run things:

- **An event** — something on a date, once or every week, with tickets people book: a run, a class, a meetup.
- **A membership** — a pass people buy: a monthly unlimited plan, a 10-class pack, a season pass.
- **A venue** — a place with courts, pitches or rooms that people book by the slot.

The wizard then takes you through that one; the steps are below. You can add the others any time from the sidebar, or click **Set up something else too** on the wizard's last step to come back to this choice. **Back** on the first step of each path returns here too.

### Getting help from Circls

From this step on, the wizard shows **Get help from Circls**. Click it if you'd like the Circls team to set things up with you, right here in your portal — it lets them in for 3 hours, and you can add a note about what you need. See [Getting help from Circls](/help/team) for what they can and can't do, and how to end it early.

## If you start with an event

You get the same form as **Events → New event** (see [Creating and publishing events](/help/events)). Only a **name** is needed to save a draft; dates, location and ticket tiers can follow.

- A new organisation has no venue yet, so **Where** starts on **No venue — enter address**, with **Country** set to your organisation's and the **Timezone** to match: India's, or in the US your browser's own (Eastern if your browser is elsewhere). Event times are read in that timezone, so check it before you enter dates. If you added a venue in the wizard first, the event starts at that venue instead, in its timezone.
- Click **Save draft**. **Back** can't be used while it saves. The last step confirms your event is saved as a draft. Click **Open your event** to finish it — anything you left for later, then **Submit for review** on the event's page — or **Finish — go to dashboard**.

Circls approves every event before customers can see it.

## If you start with a membership

You get the same form as **Memberships → New plan** (see [Setting up membership plans](/help/memberships)).

- **Save as draft** keeps the plan to yourself — it needs only a name, and its tiers can follow. Finish it on the plan's page and click **Submit for review** there.
- **Create plan** sends it straight to review; it needs at least one tier.

The last step says which it was. Click **Open your plan** to see it, or **Finish — go to dashboard**. Circls approves every plan before customers can see it.

## If you start with a venue

A venue takes three short steps: the venue, its first arena, and a schedule.

### Add your first venue

A **venue** is a physical location (a club, a complex, a studio).

1. Enter a **Venue name** (for example, *Greenfield Main Campus*).
2. Optionally add **tags** (such as `indoor` or `premium`) to describe it.
3. Click **Add venue** — or **Back** to choose something else.

New venues are created with the status **pending review**. The circls team reviews each listing before it becomes visible to consumers. You can add the address, map location and photos afterwards — see [Managing venues and arenas](/help/venues).

### Add your first arena

An **arena** is a bookable space inside a venue — a court, a pool, a turf, a studio room.

1. Enter an **Arena name** (for example, *Court 1*).
2. Optionally pick a **Sport** (Badminton, Basketball, Cricket, Football, Padel, Pickleball, Squash, Table Tennis, Tennis, Volleyball, or Other). If you leave it blank, circls tries to infer the sport from your tags.
3. Optionally add **tags**.
4. Click **Add arena** — or **Skip for now**.

Like venues, arenas start as **pending review**. Each arena has a default slot length of 60 minutes, which you can change in the schedule builder.

### Set up a schedule

The last step links you to the schedule builder, where you define opening hours and release bookable slots.

- Click **Open schedule builder** to jump straight in, or **Finish — go to dashboard** to do it later.
- If you skipped the arena step there's nothing to schedule yet: click **Go to dashboard**, and build schedules any time from the Venues page once the venue has an arena.

For the full walkthrough, see [Setting up schedules and slot pricing](/help/schedule).

## What happens next

After onboarding you land on your **dashboard**, which shows bookings, revenue and occupancy once they start coming in. Whatever you set up sits in **draft** or **pending review** until the circls team approves it — only then does it appear to consumers and become bookable.

While you wait for approval you can keep building: add events, membership plans, venues and arenas, upload photos, configure schedules, and invite your team.
